Cargo Vault One has no yield strategy contract. Capital is not deployed by code; it buys shares that are already backed by receipted cocoa. The on-chain logic that matters is how shares are sold and how they come back.

## Genesis curve

A primary sale by the Vault SPV on a linear price curve.

```text
P(R) = 4.793 + (5.118 − 4.793) × R / 1,500,000      R = USDC raised so far
Shares for a ticket D = (1/b) × ln( P(R + D) / P(R) ),   b = 0.325 / 1,500,000
```

Each ticket pays the average price across its slice of the curve, so splitting a ticket gives exactly the same number of shares. Fills are ordered by time, the priority window first. Below the $500,000 minimum, all USDC is refunded; the cap is $1,500,000.

## Batch auction

| Step | Rule |
| :-- | :-- |
| Seller | The Vault SPV only, against newly receipted lots |
| Bids | Sealed limit bids (price, quantity), minimum $5,000, editable until clearing |
| Clearing | Every 12 days at 12:00 UTC; uniform price at the lowest winning bid; pro rata at the margin |
| Settlement | Atomic: USDC against shares |
| Fees | 0.25% each side; panel market makers fee-free |
| Tape | Clearing price, discount to NAV, volume, bid-to-cover and lot IDs, emitted at clearing |
| Errors | Reversed only for system error, within one hour of clearing |

## Creation and redemption

| Flow | Who | Price | Settlement |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Creation | Authorised participants | NAV + 0.10% | Blocks of 1,000 shares, any business day, T+1 in USDC |
| Redemption | Any holder from 1,000 shares | NAV − 0.10% | Requested until 24 h before a batch; struck at the batch; paid within 12 days |

## Safeguards

<Warning>
  Creations and redemptions pause when the NAV fallback triggers. Minting stops if the collateral-manager report is stale or a lot shows a discrepancy above 0.5%. Every contract that mints, auctions or holds value is audited before mainnet.
</Warning>
