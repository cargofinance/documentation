Cargo Finance is a product by **Nova**. Nova Commodities originates, warehouses and exports physical soft commodities from Cameroon. Cycle 1 runs through Douala; Nova also works the ports of Limbe and Kribi. Cargo Finance funds that trade against the goods themselves and puts the result on-chain as **Cargo Vault One**.

Repayment is the sale of the financed goods, not a refinancing and not a new investor.

## Where Cargo fits in DeFi

On-chain warehouse lending is already proven. In July 2026, Grove committed a $500 million senior secured warehouse lending facility to Galaxy, funded on-chain. But the eligible collateral in that facility, as in almost every on-chain credit vault of scale, is BTC and ETH: the yield is still a claim on crypto price risk, closely correlated with the rest of a crypto book. Cargo's collateral is a kilogram of cocoa with a receipt, a pledge, an inspector and a daily exchange-linked price. Its return comes from the physical spread between the port and export, not from leverage or token incentives.

## Track record

Nova's first cycle closed before Vault One launched: **1,020 tonnes** of cocoa acquired in June 2026, then shipped and sold on 14 July 2026, priced against ICE London.
