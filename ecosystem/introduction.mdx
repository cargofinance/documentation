Cargo Finance is a product by **Nova**. Nova Commodities originates, warehouses and exports physical soft commodities from Cameroon through Douala. Cargo Finance funds that trade against the goods themselves and puts the result on-chain as **Cargo Vault One**.

Repayment is the sale of the financed goods, not a refinancing and not a new investor.

## Who does what

| Role | Who |
| :-- | :-- |
| Vault SPV (issuer, owner of the goods) | A bankruptcy-remote Seychelles SPV with no other business and limited recourse |
| Sponsor and issuer group | Nova Commodities Trading Ltd, Seychelles IBC 250153 |
| Originator and operator | Nova Commodities Cameroon SARL, Yaoundé and Douala (RCCM CM-NSI-01-2026-B13-00030) |
| Collateral manager | Independent inspector controlling the bay at AGL Bonabéri, Douala; sole release authority |
| Stock verification | Vallis inspects, weighs and grades each lot |
| Security agent | Holds one OHADA pledge for all holders |
| Authorised participants and market makers | Panel firms that create, redeem and quote cvCOCOA/USDC as principals |
| Liquidity Bridge LPs | Provide USDC so holders can exit instantly at NAV − 1.0% |
| Originators | ONCC-registered exporters and cooperatives that list receipted lots and keep 10% first loss |
| Holders | Eligible non-US professional and institutional investors, KYC'd and whitelisted |

The trustee, bank and auditor are named on signing.

## Where Cargo fits in DeFi

On-chain warehouse lending is already proven, but the collateral in most large on-chain credit facilities is still BTC and ETH: the yield is a claim on crypto price risk. Cargo's collateral is a kilogram of cocoa with a receipt, a pledge, an inspector and a daily exchange-linked price. Its return comes from the physical spread between the port and export, not from leverage or token incentives.

## Track record

Nova's first cycle closed before Vault One launched: **1,020 tonnes** of cocoa acquired in June 2026, then shipped and sold on 14 July 2026, priced against ICE London.
