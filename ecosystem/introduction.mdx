Cargo Finance is a product by **Nova**. Cargo funds the physical trade in real-world assets against the goods themselves and puts the result on-chain as vaults: soft commodities such as cocoa, coffee and sugar, precious metals such as gold, and other goods that can be receipted, stored under independent control and priced off a published reference.

Repayment is the sale of the financed goods, not a refinancing and not a new investor.

## Who does what

Every Cargo vault has the same set of roles. The right-hand column shows who fills each role in the Genesis Vault (Cameroon cocoa, Douala).

| Role | What it does | In the Genesis Vault |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Vault SPV | Issuer and owner of the goods; bankruptcy-remote, no other business, limited recourse | A Seychelles SPV |
| Sponsor and issuer group | Sets up and sponsors each vault | Nova Commodities Trading Ltd, Seychelles IBC 250153, 306 Victoria House, Victoria, Mahé |
| Originator and operator | Buys the goods, delivers them into custody, keeps 10% first loss | Nova Commodities Cameroon SARL, Yaoundé and Douala (RCCM CM-NSI-01-2026-B13-00030) |
| Collateral manager | Independent controller of the warehouse or vault; sole release authority | Independent inspector controlling the bay at AGL Bonabéri, Douala |
| Stock verification | Inspects, weighs and grades each lot | Vallis |
| Security agent | Holds one pledge for all holders | Named on signing |
| Authorised participants and market makers | Create, redeem and quote vault shares against USDC as principals | Panel firms |
| Liquidity Bridge LPs | Provide USDC so holders can exit instantly at NAV − 1.0% | Panel LPs |
| Additional originators | Approved producers, exporters or dealers that list receipted lots and keep 10% first loss | ONCC-registered exporters and cooperatives |
| Holders | Eligible non-US professional and institutional investors, KYC'd and whitelisted | Same for every vault |

The trustee, bank and auditor for each vault are named on signing.

## Where Cargo fits in DeFi

On-chain warehouse lending is already proven. In July 2026, Grove committed a $500 million senior secured warehouse lending facility to Galaxy, funded on-chain. But the eligible collateral in that facility, as in almost every on-chain credit vault of scale, is BTC and ETH: the yield is still a claim on crypto price risk, closely correlated with the rest of a crypto book. Cargo's collateral is a physical unit of a real-world asset (a kilogram of cocoa, a tonne of sugar, an ounce of gold) with a receipt, a pledge, an inspector and a daily reference price. Its return comes from the physical spread between origin and market, not from leverage or token incentives.

## Track record

The Genesis Vault's originator, Nova, closed its first cycle before Vault One launched: **1,020 tonnes** of Cameroon cocoa acquired in June 2026, then shipped and sold on 14 July 2026, priced against ICE London.
