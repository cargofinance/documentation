Cargo Finance is a decentralized finance (DeFi) protocol connecting on-chain capital with real-world investment opportunities across Africa and emerging markets.

Through **Real-World Asset (RWA) Vaults**, Cargo provides access to curated private credit and asset-backed yield strategies, bridging traditional finance and decentralized markets.

## Ecosystem Overview

Cargo's ecosystem connects investors, capital allocators, and real-world asset originators through on-chain vaults. Each vault deploys capital into selected strategies backed by real-world economic activity, generating yield through financing returns and profit-sharing arrangements.

## Key Participants

- **Depositors:** Investors providing capital to RWA Vaults.
- **Strategy Operators:** Participants managing capital allocation and investment strategies.
- **Asset Originators:** Partners sourcing and structuring real-world financing opportunities.
- **Custodians & Legal Partners:** Entities supporting asset security, compliance, and off-chain enforceability.
- **Governance Participants:** Stakeholders overseeing protocol parameters and strategic decisions, where governance mechanisms apply.

## Where Cargo Fits in DeFi

Unlike traditional DeFi protocols that rely primarily on crypto-native lending and incentives, **Cargo connects decentralized capital to real-world economic activity.**

By combining curated asset-backed strategies, on-chain transparency, and structured risk management, Cargo aims to make emerging-market private credit more accessible, transparent, and capital-efficient.
