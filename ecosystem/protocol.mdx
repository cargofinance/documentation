Cargo Finance connects on-chain capital with real-world yield-generating assets through a structured vault architecture. The protocol facilitates capital allocation, yield distribution, and transparent revenue management across its ecosystem.

## Protocol Architecture

Cargo operates through **RWA Vault smart contracts** that receive investor deposits and allocate capital to curated investment strategies. Strategy contracts manage the on-chain allocation and accounting of capital deployed into real-world assets, while protocol-level contracts coordinate vault operations, fee collection, and distributions.

## Revenue and Fee Flow

Revenue is generated through underlying real-world financing activities, including private credit interest, financing fees, and profit-sharing arrangements.

As strategies generate returns, applicable protocol fees are deducted from realized yield, with the remaining returns allocated to depositors according to their vault participation.

Protocol revenue may be directed to the **Cargo Treasury** to support ecosystem development, operations, and long-term sustainability.
