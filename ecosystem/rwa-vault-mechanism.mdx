RWA Vaults are the core product of Cargo Finance. They accept crypto deposits, allocate capital to curated real-world asset strategies, and generate potential yield for depositors while maintaining on-chain transparency.

## How RWA Vaults Work

Depositors provide capital through smart contract vaults, typically using stablecoins. Funds are pooled and allocated to selected strategies financing real-world economic activities, including private credit and trade finance.

As underlying assets generate returns, yield is attributed to depositors based on their vault participation, after applicable fees.

## Asset Backing

Cargo Vaults are designed to provide exposure to real-world financing opportunities backed by identifiable assets, contractual receivables, or collateral.

Underlying investments may include:

- **Private Credit:** Loans to businesses and institutional borrowers.
- **Trade Finance:** Financing backed by trade receivables and commercial transactions.
- **Asset-Backed Financing:** Credit secured against physical or financial assets.

Assets are originated primarily across Africa and emerging markets, with legal ownership, custody, and investor claims determined by each vault's structure and jurisdiction.

## On-Chain vs. Off-Chain Components

| Component | On-Chain | Off-Chain |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Deposits & Withdrawals | Smart contract vaults | Fiat settlement, where required |
| Ownership Records | Tokenized vault positions | Legal agreements and investor records |
| Asset Management | Strategy allocation and accounting | Origination, servicing, and custody |
| Transparency & NAV | Vault reporting and valuation updates | Asset verification and financial reporting |
| Yield Distribution | Smart contract accounting and distributions | Interest payments and asset cash flows |

## NAV and Pricing

**Net Asset Value (NAV)** represents the total value of a vault's underlying assets, including accrued income, less liabilities and applicable fees.

## Redemption

Depositors request withdrawals through the Cargo Vault interface. Redemption is processed according to the vault's available liquidity and investment terms.

Depending on the underlying strategy, vaults may include:

- **Lock-Up Periods:** Defined investment durations, potentially 90, 180, or 360 days.
- **Redemption Windows:** Scheduled opportunities to withdraw capital.
- **Liquidity Buffers:** Available reserves to support eligible withdrawals.

Withdrawal timing and liquidity depend on the underlying assets, repayment schedules, and vault-specific conditions. Returns and principal are not guaranteed.

<Warning>
  Real-world asset strategies may involve settlement delays or liquidity constraints that differ from standard DeFi vaults. Review the vault-specific terms before depositing.
</Warning>
