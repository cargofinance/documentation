Protocol updates, document versions and launch milestones for Cargo Vault One. Dates are in UTC.

<Update label="24 Oct 2026" description="Docs refresh">
  Documentation rewritten for the v5 design: cvCOCOA vault shares, ONCC-based NAV, the Genesis curve, 12-day batch auctions, the Liquidity Bridge and the fee schedule. FAQs completed.
</Update>

<Update label="Oct 2026" description="Genesis Auction rescheduled">
  The Genesis Auction for Cycle 1, Cameroon cocoa, originally planned for 14–23 Oct 2026, has been rescheduled. New dates will be announced here and on the official channels. The terms published in Launch Pack v5.0 (curve from $4.793 to $5.118 per share, $500,000 minimum, $1,500,000 cap) may be updated with the new dates.
</Update>

<Update label="11 Oct 2026" description="Launch Pack v5.0">
  Direct allocation into receipted cocoa, priced off ONCC. The KG unit is renamed **cvCOCOA** (Cargo Vault Cocoa · Douala). NAV becomes ONCC's FOB reference less export levy and FOB costs. The v4 coupon tiers, 8-day rotations and Sun 11 → Sun 18 Oct dates are withdrawn. New documents: Market Maker Offer (CV1-MM) and Liquidity Provider and Listing Offer (CV1-LPL).
</Update>

<Update label="10 Oct 2026" description="Whitepaper v4.0">
  Public whitepaper (CV1-WP) published: the collateral object, the backing rule, proof of reserve, haircuts, the curve mathematics and the eight tests for any commodity RWA. The mechanism holds under v5; its economics are being re-based.
</Update>

<Update label="16 Sep 2026" description="Arc mainnet">
  Circle's USDC-native chain, Arc, launched mainnet. Arc is the target home for cvCOCOA, with Base as the fallback.
</Update>

<Update label="14 Jul 2026" description="First cycle closed">
  Nova's first cycle closed: 1,020 t of cocoa acquired in June 2026, shipped and sold against ICE London.
</Update>

<Info>
  **Coming up:** after Genesis settles, a batch auction every 12 days, starting with Batch 2. First redemptions strike at Batch 4. A permissioned cvCOCOA/USDC pool is planned from Batch 2 and a Morpho lending market after Batch 4. Dates follow the rescheduled Genesis and will be confirmed in the term sheet.
</Info>
