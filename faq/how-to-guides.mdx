## Complete KYC and whitelist a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Complete Persona KYC (individuals: ID, liveness, proof of address) or KYB (entities: incorporation documents, directors, beneficial owners above 10%, signatory authority). Sign the Eligibility Declaration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register wallets">
    Send up to three wallet addresses and their chain to compliance. You receive a participant ID and a nonce.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the ownership message">
    Sign this message from each wallet:

    ```text
    Cargo Vault One — wallet ownership
    I, [legal name / entity], control this wallet and register it for Cargo Vault One.
    Participant ID: [CV1-P-0000]
    Chain: [Arc / Base]
    Date (UTC): [YYYY-MM-DD]
    Nonce: [issued by compliance]
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    After screening, the wallet is added to the identity registry and you receive a confirmation. Screening re-runs on new orders and every six months. Tell compliance about any new beneficial owner, country of residence or wallet before your next trade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify a deposit address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read it from your agreement">The address is printed in your signed agreement. Ignore any address sent by chat, DM or email.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the signed announcement">The same address is posted, signed by the issuer key, on the official announcement channel.</Step>
  <Step title="Call a named director">Use a phone number from the data room, not from a message. The director reads you the first 6 and last 4 characters.</Step>
  <Step title="Send a test amount">Send a small amount first (for example 10 USDC) and wait for confirmation before sending the balance.</Step>
</Steps>

## Bid in a batch auction

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the lots">Check the published lot IDs, tonnage, grade, receipts and reserves, and today's NAV.</Step>
  <Step title="Place a sealed bid">Enter price per share and quantity (minimum $5,000). You can edit it until clearing.</Step>
  <Step title="Settle">At 12:00 UTC on the batch day, everyone filled pays the same clearing price, plus 0.25%. USDC and shares swap atomically.</Step>
</Steps>

## Redeem at NAV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request">From 1,000 shares, submit a redemption request at least 24 hours before a batch.</Step>
  <Step title="Strike">Your request strikes at that batch's NAV, less 0.10%.</Step>
  <Step title="Paid">USDC is paid on delivery, within 12 days of the strike. The first redemptions strike at Batch 4, Mon 30 Nov 2026.</Step>
</Steps>

## Exit instantly through the Liquidity Bridge

Sell shares to the bridge at NAV − 1.0%, up to its published capacity. Check live capacity before you sell.

## Take physical delivery

From 10,000 shares (10 t), request delivery ex-warehouse Douala to your ONCC-registered exporter. You pay cost, and receive the collateral manager's weight and quality certificates.
