<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't you show an APY?">
    cvCOCOA pays no coupon. Its return is the discount to NAV you buy at, plus or minus NAV moves, plus any Genesis lock bonus. Those depend on prices and on how often you rotate, so annualising them would mislead. Older materials quoting 18%, 28% or 35% APY described an earlier facility structure and no longer apply.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is NAV calculated?">
    NAV per share = (ONCC FOB reference − export levy − FOB costs) ÷ FX. At ONCC prices of 9 Oct 2026: (3,259 − 125 − 100) FCFA/kg = 3,034 FCFA/kg, divided by 575 XAF per USD = **$5.277**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often is NAV updated?">
    NAV is struck daily after ONCC publishes and posted on-chain. iNAV, which moves NAV with ICE London cocoa between fixes, updates every minute by API and WebSocket and every 15 minutes on-chain during ICE hours. Creations and redemptions strike at NAV.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does NAV change?">
    - The ONCC FOB reference price of cocoa moves.
    - EUR/USD moves (the CFA franc is pegged to the euro).
    - FOB costs are re-based quarterly on invoices, or at once if realised sales run $150/t below NAV for 30 days.
    - The export levy changes only if the law changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the discount, and why does it change?">
    It is how far below NAV new shares sell. On the Genesis curve it runs from 9.2% to 3.0%; in each batch auction it is set by competing sealed bids. It will compress as more capital arrives.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if ONCC and ICE disagree?">
    If the ONCC-implied differential to ICE moves more than $250/t from its 30-day median, or ONCC does not publish for two business days, NAV falls back to ICE plus the median differential and creations and redemptions pause for that batch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I borrow against my shares?">
    A cvCOCOA/USDC Morpho market is planned after Batch 4, at a 62.5% liquidation LTV with a lower-of NAV oracle. It is not live yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  All figures are at ONCC prices of 9 Oct 2026, before price moves. They are illustrations, not forecasts. Capital is at risk.
</Note>
