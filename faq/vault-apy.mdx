Cargo Finance Vaults generate potential yield through real-world investment strategies, including private credit, trade finance, and asset-backed financing. Each vault displays an estimated Annual Percentage Yield (APY) to help depositors evaluate potential returns.

## APY vs. APR

**Annual Percentage Yield (APY)** represents the estimated annual return on an investment, including the effects of compounding.

**Annual Percentage Rate (APR)** represents the annualized return without compounding.

Cargo uses APY as its target yield metric. Where compounding is supported, projected APY reflects the applicable reinvestment frequency. Otherwise, returns are presented on a non-compounded basis.

## Yield Sources

Vault returns are generated through underlying real-world economic activities, including:

- **Private Credit:** Interest earned from business lending and structured financing.
- **Trade Finance:** Returns generated through short-term commercial financing and receivables.
- **Profit Sharing:** Additional returns linked to the profitability of eligible underlying transactions.
- **Protocol Incentives:** Additional rewards, where applicable, reported separately from underlying asset yield.

Displayed net APY accounts for applicable management and performance fees, based on each vault's fee structure.

## Why Does APY Change?

Vault APY may fluctuate due to:

- **Market Conditions:** Changes in lending rates and credit demand.
- **Asset Performance:** Borrower repayments, defaults, and financing profitability.
- **Capital Allocation:** Changes in vault utilization and available investment opportunities.
- **Reinvestment:** Compounding frequency and reinvestment availability.
- **Protocol Fees:** Changes to applicable management or performance fees.

Short-term APY fluctuations do not necessarily reflect long-term performance. Depositors should consider the vault's underlying assets, risk profile, and investment duration when evaluating projected returns.

_APY figures are estimates, not guaranteed returns. Actual performance may differ due to credit risk, market conditions, liquidity constraints, and underlying asset performance._
