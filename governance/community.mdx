## How to Contribute

Cargo Finance welcomes participation from investors, researchers, developers, and members of the broader DeFi community.

- **Community Engagement:** Join discussions, share feedback, and help shape the ecosystem.
- **Research & Content:** Contribute insights on RWA markets, private credit, and decentralized finance.
- **Governance:** Participate in protocol discussions and governance proposals as these mechanisms become available.
- **Development:** Explore opportunities to contribute to Cargo's technical infrastructure and integrations.
- **Ecosystem Growth:** Introduce potential partners, asset originators, and collaborators.
