- **Research:** share data or views on cocoa, trade finance and RWA market structure.
- **Introductions:** originators, offtakers, market makers and venues.
- **Feedback:** tell us what would make the docs or the product clearer.

## Community guidelines

<Warning>
  Cargo will never send you a deposit address by chat, DM or email alone, and will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys. The only valid deposit address is the one in your signed agreement, confirmed by phone with a named director. Treat any other address as a scam.
</Warning>
