Cargo has **no governance token**, and vault shares carry no voting rights. Holders are protected by contract, by the structure and by published rules rather than by votes.

## Who decides what

| Decision | Who | Check |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Haircuts, caps, eligible buyers and lots | Each vault's risk committee: the sponsor, the independent director, the security agent as observer (Nova sponsors the Genesis Vault) | Published to eligible investors |
| Releases of goods | Collateral manager | Only against payment |
| Treasury and contract administration | Safe multisig with a timelock | Independent co-signer; see [Contracts & Timelocks](/safety/contracts-and-timelocks) |
| Rulebook and fee changes | Issuer | 30 days' notice, except safety fixes |
| Safety pauses | Pause guardian | Immediate; reason published |

## How a rule changes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">The change and its reason are written up against the Rulebook.</Step>
  <Step title="Notice">Eligible investors receive 30 days' notice before it takes effect.</Step>
  <Step title="Effect">The new Rulebook is published and its hash recorded.</Step>
</Steps>

Safety fixes, such as pausing a batch after an oracle failure, a collateral-manager discrepancy above 0.5%, a sanctions hit or a chain incident, take effect immediately.

## Conflicts of interest

A vault's sponsor can also be its originator and servicer. In the Genesis Vault, Nova is all three. To manage that, in every vault:

- The sponsor never bids in its own lots.
- Bids are sealed and cleared by contract.
- Reserves are published before each batch window opens.
- An independent reviewer checks auction logs quarterly.

## Reporting

| Item | Cadence |
| :-- | :-- |
| NAV | Daily, on-chain |
| Collateral manager stock report hash | Weekly |
| Batch tape | Each batch |
| Investor letter | Monthly |
| Agreed-upon procedures on stock against shares | Quarterly |
| Financial statement audit | Annual |
