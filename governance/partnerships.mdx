Cargo works with independent firms for custody, verification, identity and settlement, and runs open programmes for market makers, liquidity providers, originators and venues. Each vault adds its own local counterparties: a warehouse or vault operator, an inspector, a bank and a price reference.

## Operating counterparties

| Area | Every vault | In the Genesis Vault |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Storage | A warehouse or vault under an independent collateral manager | Approved bonded warehouse |
| Stock verification | An independent inspector for every lot | Vallis |
| Banking | A bank for sale proceeds and payments to originators | Partner bank, named on signing |
| Price reference | A published reference price and a hedge market; not partners | ICE London Cocoa |
| Identity | Persona, for KYC and KYB | Persona |

The trustee, security agent, auditor and insurer for each vault are named to investors on signing.

## Approved buyers (offtake)

Processors, exporters, refiners and traders who want to buy the goods in a Cargo vault join that vault's offtake panel. On top of entity KYB, an approved buyer provides:

- two trade references;
- a signed Approved Buyer Agreement and evidence of authority to sign contracts;
- import and shipping details for the destination;
- bank details for refunds of deposits.

## Listing on other private venues

We want Cargo vault tokens wherever eligible buyers already trade, as long as the venue keeps the whitelist and the NAV anchor intact.

| A venue qualifies if it | We provide |
| :-- | :-- |
| Is licensed for the activity where it operates | The NAV and iNAV feed, free |
| Enforces the ERC-3643 whitelist, or routes transfers through it | Authorised-participant access for the venue's market makers |
| Onboards only eligible investors | The lot registry and collateral-manager reports |
| Shows NAV and the premium or discount next to the price | Listing support and joint announcements |
| Settles atomically and reports trades to the tape | No listing fees either way |

Permissioned RFQ networks and licensed OTC desks can quote now, as principals or agents under their own licences. A regulated EU DLT venue becomes worthwhile once monthly volume and EU demand justify it.
