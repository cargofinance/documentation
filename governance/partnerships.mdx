Cargo works with independent firms for custody, verification, identity and settlement, and runs open programmes for market makers, liquidity providers, originators and venues.

## Operating counterparties

| Area | Counterparty | Role |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Port terminal | AGL Bonabéri, Douala | Bay where receipted lots are stored |
| Stock verification | Vallis | Inspects, weighs and grades every lot |
| Banking | Access Bank Cameroon | Export domiciliation and proceeds |
| Price reference | ONCC (published daily price) and ICE London cocoa | NAV input and hedge reference; not partners |
| Identity | Persona | KYC and KYB |

The trustee, security agent, auditor and insurer are named to investors on signing.

## Partner programmes

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Market makers and APs" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Quote cvCOCOA/USDC around iNAV, answer RFQs and bid in every batch. In return: authorised-participant status, an inventory loan, fee rebates and a share of auction fees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Liquidity Bridge LPs" icon="bridge">
    Buy exits at NAV − 1.0% and redeem at NAV − 0.10%, in a segregated mandate. Minimum $50,000.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Originators" icon="warehouse">
    ONCC-registered exporters and cooperatives list receipted lots of 25 t or more and sell at export parity less the clearing discount, instead of at the Douala bid.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private venues" icon="building-columns">
    Licensed venues can list cvCOCOA with our NAV feed, whitelist and APs. No listing fees either way.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
