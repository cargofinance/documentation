Cargo Finance is a product by Nova.

## Mission

Make the physical asset itself (a kilogram, a tonne, an ounce) into collateral that travels, so that on-chain capital can finance the first mile of the real-world asset trade against goods that are checked, owned, pledged and priced, rather than against crypto price risk.

## Roadmap

| When | Step |
| :-- | :-- |
| Jul 2026 | Genesis Vault originator's first cycle closed: 1,020 t of West African Grade 1 cocoa bought, shipped and sold |
| Rescheduled, dates to be announced | Genesis Auction for the Genesis Vault (Cycle 1): $500k minimum, $1.5M cap |

| Batch 4 | First redemptions |

| At scale | A licensed secondary venue (such as a regulated DLT venue) once volume justifies it |
