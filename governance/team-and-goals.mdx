Cargo Finance is a product by Nova. The team combines physical commodity origination in Cameroon with capital-markets and blockchain experience.

## Team

- **Yves Guillaume A. Messy, Founder and CEO.** Head Tutor on the Oxford Blockchain Strategy Programme and a mentor at the F10 Fintech Incubator, with expertise in Rust and Move and in capital markets.
- **Origination and operations:** Nova Commodities Cameroon SARL, in Yaoundé and Douala, buys, warehouses and exports the goods.
- **Leadership roles:** ecosystem, product, revenue, growth and marketing, and business development.
- **Independent roles:** an independent director on the Vault SPV, the collateral manager and the security agent sit outside Nova.

Cargo operates from Zug (Switzerland), Mahé (Seychelles) and Yaoundé and Douala (Cameroon).

## Mission

Make the kilogram itself into collateral that travels, so that on-chain capital can finance the first mile of the commodity trade against goods that are checked, owned, pledged and priced, rather than against crypto price risk.
