Cargo's revenue comes from running the venue, not from a share of holders' cocoa. Holder assets and Cargo's operating money never mix.

## Revenue sources

| Source | Rate |
| :-- | :-- |
| Listing and servicing, paid by originators | 0.50% of NAV, once per lot |
| Batch auction | 0.25% each side |
| Creation and redemption | 0.10% |

The 1.0% Liquidity Bridge fee goes to bridge LPs, not to Cargo. Storage, collateral management and insurance pass through at cost. Over a lot's life of about 45 days, venue fees come to about **1.35%** of the lot. Part of gross auction fees is shared with panel market makers and venue investors under their agreements.

## Ring-fencing

- The Vault SPV owns the goods and has no other business and no other creditors.
- Vault goods are never pledged or financed anywhere else, and never rehypothecated.
- Cargo's operating runway is raised separately, as equity in the issuer group, and never touches holder collateral.
- Settlement is non-custodial: USDC and shares swap atomically between wallets. Cargo does not hold client assets.

## Controls

- Treasury and contract administration run through a **Safe multisig with a timelock**, with an independent co-signer required on transfers out.
- Originators are paid in EUR or XAF into domiciled CEMAC bank accounts, never in USDC, under BEAC exchange rules.

## Transparency and reporting

<Note>
  The Safe and contract addresses are published on the [Contract Addresses](/resources/contract-addresses) page. Batch results go on the public tape at clearing. Investors receive a monthly letter, quarterly agreed-upon procedures on stock against shares, and annual audited financial statements.
</Note>
