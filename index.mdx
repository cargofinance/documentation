Cargo Finance is a decentralized protocol that lets you earn yield by depositing crypto into on-chain vaults backed by real-world assets. These docs explain how the protocol works, how to use it, and how to stay safe while interacting with smart contracts.

## What is Cargo Finance?

Real World Assets and emerging market Private Credit yield on-chain. We originate, allocate and curate RWA vaults.

## What is the RWA Vault mechanism?

A Real-World Asset (RWA) Vault is a blockchain-based investment structure that enables investors to access yield-generating opportunities backed by real-world assets, such as private credit, trade finance, and other asset-backed financing arrangements.

RWA Vaults bridge traditional finance and decentralized finance (DeFi) by bringing real-world investment opportunities on-chain. Through smart contracts, vaults can facilitate capital allocation, track investor positions, and manage distributions according to predefined investment terms.

### How Does It Work?

Investors deposit eligible digital assets, such as stablecoins, into a vault. The pooled capital is then allocated to selected real-world financing opportunities, where returns are generated through underlying economic activities, such as loan repayments, financing fees, or revenue-sharing arrangements.

As the underlying investments generate income, returns are attributed to vault participants according to the vault's terms and performance.

### How Does Cargo Finance Use RWA Vaults?

Cargo Finance connects investors with curated private credit and real-world asset opportunities across Africa and emerging markets. Its vault-based model is designed to simplify access to traditionally fragmented financial markets through structured, on-chain investment products.

By combining asset origination, due diligence, capital allocation, and blockchain-based infrastructure, Cargo aims to provide investors with greater transparency into how capital is deployed and how returns are generated.

### Key Benefits

- Accessibility: Exposure to private credit and real-world financing opportunities through digital assets.
- Transparency: On-chain visibility into vault transactions and capital movements.
- Diversification: Access to financing opportunities across different assets, sectors, and markets.
- Yield Generation: Potential returns linked to underlying real-world economic activity.
- Automated Infrastructure: Smart contracts support investment accounting, allocation, and distribution processes.

Important: RWA Vaults are not risk-free. Returns depend on the performance of the underlying assets, and investments may be exposed to borrower defaults, liquidity constraints, smart contract vulnerabilities, and market risks. On-chain transparency does not automatically guarantee the performance or security of off-chain assets.
