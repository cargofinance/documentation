In every Cargo vault, new shares are sold only by the Vault SPV, below NAV. You hold them in your own whitelisted wallet. There are four ways out. The figures below are the Genesis Vault's (cvCOCOA, West African Grade 1 cocoa); each new vault publishes its own in its term sheet.

## Buying

### Genesis Auction

Each vault opens with a Genesis Auction: a bonding curve that stops below NAV. The price rises with the USDC raised, so earlier money buys at a deeper discount.

**Genesis Vault (Cycle 1).** Price per share **P(R) = 4.793 + (5.118 − 4.793) × R ÷ 1,500,000**, where R is USDC raised so far.

### Batch Auctions, Every 12 Days

After its Genesis Auction, each vault sells new shares in sealed batch auctions at a fixed interval. In the Genesis Vault, the interval is 12 days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lots Are Published">
    Lot ID, quantity, grade, warehouse, receipt, originator and reserve are published before the window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a Sealed Bid">
    Price per share and quantity, minimum $5,000. Bids can be edited until clearing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clearing">
    Every 12 days at 12:00 UTC, starting after Genesis settlement (weekends and reference-market holidays roll to the next trading day). One uniform price, the lowest winning bid, pro rata at the margin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement">
    Atomic and on-chain: USDC against shares. 0.25% fee per side; panel market makers bid fee-free. The clearing price, discount to NAV, volume, bid-to-cover and lot IDs go on the public tape.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Route | Who | Price | Timing |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Redemption** | Any holder above the vault minimum (Genesis Vault: 1,000 shares) | NAV − 0.10% | Request until 24 h before a batch; struck at that batch; paid within 12 days in the Genesis Vault |
| **Liquidity Bridge** | Any holder | NAV − 1.0% | Instant, up to published capacity |
| **RFQ** | Any holder | Firm quotes from panel market makers | From Genesis settlement; blocks from $25,000; settled atomically |
| **Physical delivery** | Above the vault minimum (Genesis Vault: 10,000 shares) | At cost | Ex-warehouse to your nominated buyer (Genesis Vault: from 10 t, to your nominated licensed exporter) |

<Warning>
  In the Genesis Vault, the first redemptions strike at **Batch 4**. If redemption requests exceed bridge capacity for two batches, capacity is raised and redemptions may be filled pro rata. There is no guarantee of a bid on RFQ.
</Warning>
