Cargo Finance connects on-chain capital with curated real-world investment opportunities across Africa and emerging markets. Understanding the underlying assets, custody arrangements, and legal structures helps depositors evaluate investment risks and potential returns.

## Asset Types

Cargo Vaults are designed to provide exposure to the following asset classes, with availability depending on the vault's investment strategy and jurisdiction.

| Asset Class | Description | Typical Duration | Vault Category |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Private Credit | Loans to businesses backed by contractual repayment obligations and, where applicable, collateral. | 6–24 months | Growth |
| Trade Finance | Short-term financing supporting import, export, and commercial trade transactions. | 30–180 days | Specialized |
| Invoice Financing | Financing secured against verified business receivables and outstanding invoices. | 30–120 days | Core / Specialized |
| Asset-Backed Lending | Credit secured by physical assets, inventory, equipment, or financial collateral. | 3–12 months | Core / Growth |
| Structured Credit | Financing arrangements combining contractual cash flows, collateral, and defined repayment structures. | 6–24 months | Growth / Specialized |

_Durations are indicative and may differ by asset, borrower, and investment structure._
