Cargo Vault One holds **exchange-grade soft commodities** at the port: goods with a standard grade, a deep reference price and a short, self-liquidating path to export. Cycle 1 is Cameroon cocoa.

## Why soft commodities make good collateral

- **Standard grade.** A graded bean means the same thing in Douala, Antwerp and Shanghai.
- **Deep reference price.** ICE London gives a daily mark and a hedge.
- **Warehouse culture.** Collateral managers, receipts and pledges are routine in the physical trade.
- **Self-liquidating.** Goods are bought to be sold; collateral turns into cash within weeks.
- **Wide demand.** Grinders, exporters and traders buy every week.

What makes them hard is volatility, basis between the physical and futures prices, quality decay and legal reach. Cargo's structure is built around those four problems.

## Custody and verification

- **Collateral manager:** independent, with sole release authority over the bay. Goods leave only against payment.
- **Verification:** Vallis inspects, weighs and grades every lot. A discrepancy above 0.5% on any lot suspends it until it is topped up or met from the originator's subordinated shares, and triggers an independent recount.
- **Reporting:** weekly stock reports hashed on-chain; lot IDs, weights and grades public.
- **Insurance:** all-risks stock cover on every lot.

## Eight tests for any commodity

A commodity becomes a Cargo vault only if it passes all eight:

1. **Standard grade:** an exchange or trade-association specification.
2. **Deep reference price:** a liquid future or a published index.
3. **Storability:** shelf life well beyond the holding period, with known decay.
4. **Independent custody:** a collateral manager with sole release authority.
5. **Perfectable security:** a registrable pledge or title where the goods are stored.
6. **Liquid physical exit:** at least three approved buyers able to take a lot within 10 days.
7. **Insurable:** all-risk stock and transit cover at commercial rates.
8. **Traceable:** lot-level origin documents, including EUDR data where required.
