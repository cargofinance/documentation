Each Cargo Vault follows a defined investment strategy governing capital allocation, yield distribution, and portfolio management. Strategies are established at launch and may be adjusted through governance to reflect changing market conditions.

## Strategy Overview

Each strategy defines the vault's target asset allocation, investment duration, risk exposure, and reinvestment approach.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Allocation Rules">
    Capital is allocated across curated real-world asset opportunities, including private credit, trade finance, and asset-backed financing.

    - **Target Allocation:** Defined individually for each vault.
    - **Counterparty Exposure:** Limited to reduce concentration risk.
    - **Single-Asset Cap:** Maximum exposure determined by the vault's risk parameters.
    - **Liquidity Reserves:** A portion of capital may be retained to support withdrawals and operational requirements.

    Specific allocation percentages and exposure limits are published in each vault's strategy parameters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reinvestment Logic">
    Generated yield is managed according to the vault's investment terms and may be:

    - **Auto-Compounded:** Reinvested to generate additional returns.
    - **Distributed:** Paid to depositors at scheduled intervals.
    - **Reserved:** Retained to support liquidity and risk management.

    Reinvestment frequency and distribution schedules are defined at vault launch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rebalancing Triggers">
    Vault allocations may be adjusted in response to:

    - Portfolio deviations from target allocations.
    - Maturity or repayment of underlying investments.
    - Changes in borrower creditworthiness or collateral quality.
    - Market conditions and liquidity requirements.

    Rebalancing follows predefined risk limits and applicable governance approvals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Strategy Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Proposal">
    New strategies or changes to existing allocations are submitted through the [Cargo Governance](https://cargo-finance.mintlify.site/governance/governance) process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Proposals undergo risk assessment, asset due diligence, and compliance checks before approval. Reviews evaluate expected returns, collateral quality, counterparty exposure, and alignment with vault objectives.

    - **Review Period:** To be confirmed.
    - **Approval Requirements:** Defined by the governance framework.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deployment">
    Approved strategies are implemented through the relevant vault and strategy contracts, subject to applicable authorization controls.

    - **Timelock:** To be confirmed.
    - **Execution:** Authorized protocol operators or governance mechanisms.
    - **Transparency:** On-chain strategy updates and allocation records, where supported.

    All strategy changes remain subject to the vault's investment mandate and applicable risk controls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Strategy details for each vault are viewable on the app. Check the vault page for current allocation and recent rebalancing history.
</Info>
