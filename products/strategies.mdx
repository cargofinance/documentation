Cargo does not run a discretionary strategy with your money. The vault holds cocoa, one kilogram per share. What you earn depends on how you use the shares.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Hold: own the kilos" icon="weight-hanging">
    Buy cvCOCOA below NAV and keep it. Your return is the discount you bought at, plus or minus the move in NAV, which follows the ONCC export price of cocoa.

    - **Exit:** redeem at NAV − 0.10% from 1,000 shares, use the Liquidity Bridge at NAV − 1.0%, or take delivery from 10 t.
    - **Genesis locks:** buyers who lock Genesis shares for 90 or 180 days receive **+1.0%** or **+2.5%** bonus shares.
    - **Borrow (planned):** pledge your shares in a cvCOCOA/USDC Morpho market instead of selling them.

    Best for allocators who want cocoa exposure backed by receipted stock.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rotate: earn the discount every 12 days" icon="arrows-rotate">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Buy">Your bid fills at the batch clearing price, below NAV.</Step>
      <Step title="Pre-sell">The same kilos are sold to an offtaker at that day's NAV.</Step>
      <Step title="Redeem and re-bid">NAV − 0.10% comes back in USDC before the next batch.</Step>
    </Steps>

    Each turn earns roughly the clearing discount less the 0.25% auction fee and the 0.10% redemption fee. The auction sets the discount, and it will compress as capital arrives. Returns are never annualised.

    In Phase 1, Rotate runs as an **auto-roll on your own wallet**: no pool and no discretion. A pooled, compounding class (scvCOCOA) waits for the right fund route.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Make markets" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Panel market makers quote cvCOCOA/USDC around iNAV, answer RFQs, bid in every batch and act as authorised participants (create at NAV + 0.10%, redeem at NAV − 0.10%). They hedge the cocoa price on ICE London. Terms are in the Market Maker Offer (CV1-MM), available on request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bridge exits" icon="bridge">
    Liquidity Bridge LPs supply USDC that buys shares instantly from holders at NAV − 1.0% and redeems them at NAV − 0.10%, hedged on ICE. Each LP's USDC sits in its own segregated mandate, run by a licensed panel market maker; Cargo does not manage LP money. Minimum $50,000; $250,000 capacity at launch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lend (planned)" icon="hand-holding-dollar">
    An isolated cvCOCOA/USDC Morpho market: 62.5% liquidation LTV at launch, a lower-of NAV oracle and whitelisted liquidators. Lenders earn the market's borrow rate. Targeted after Batch 4.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  Speculators can take a view on NAV or clearing prices through perps, options or prediction markets on third-party venues that license Cargo's data. Those venues never hold vault shares, and the vault never takes the other side.
</Info>
