Cargo Vaults are on-chain investment products connecting digital assets with real-world yield opportunities. Each vault offers a distinct risk profile, investment duration, and asset strategy, allowing depositors to select opportunities aligned with their investment goals.

## Vault Types

We offer vaults across several risk categories.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Core Vaults">
    Core Vaults prioritize capital preservation and predictable yield through short-duration, high-quality credit instruments and collateral-backed financing.

    - **Risk Profile:** Low to moderate
    - **Investment Duration:** 90 days (indicative)
    - **Supported Assets:** USDC, USDT (subject to confirmation)
    - **Minimum Deposit:** TBD
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Growth Vaults">
    Growth Vaults target higher potential returns through diversified private credit, business financing, and emerging-market lending opportunities.

    - **Risk Profile:** Moderate
    - **Investment Duration:** 180 days (indicative)
    - **Supported Assets:** USDC, USDT (subject to confirmation)
    - **Minimum Deposit:** TBD
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Specialized Vaults">
    Specialized Vaults provide targeted exposure to opportunities such as trade finance, invoice factoring, and sector-specific asset-backed financing, with potential profit-sharing arrangements.

    - **Risk Profile:** Moderate to high
    - **Investment Duration:** 360 days (indicative)
    - **Supported Assets:** USDC, USDT (subject to confirmation)
    - **Eligibility:** May require additional KYC/KYB verification or investor qualification
    - **Minimum Deposit:** TBD
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
