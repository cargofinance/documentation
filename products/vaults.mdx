A Cargo vault holds one real-world asset as receipted, pledged inventory and issues one class of vault shares against it, with its own lots and its own NAV. Any asset that passes the [eight collateral tests](/products/real-world-assets#eight-tests-for-any-commodity) can get a vault. The first programme, **Cargo Vault One**, opens with the **Genesis Vault**: West African Grade 1 cocoa, issued as cvCOCOA.

## Every vault share

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| Name | "cv" plus the asset (for example cvCOCOA, Cargo Vault Cocoa) |
| Unit | 1 share = 1 unit of the receipted asset (a kilogram, a tonne or an ounce) |
| Owner of the goods | A bankruptcy-remote Vault SPV |
| Standard | ERC-3643 (permissioned): transfers only between whitelisted identities |
| Settlement asset | USDC |
| Custody | Holders' own wallets; administration through a Safe multisig with a timelock |
| NAV | The asset's published reference price, less levies and delivery costs, struck daily |
| Holder fees | No management or storage fee |

## The Genesis Vault: cvCOCOA at a glance

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| Name | Cargo Vault Cocoa (cvCOCOA) |
| Unit | 1 share = 1 kg of receipted cocoa |
| Asset | West African Grade 1 cocoa beans |
| Price reference | ICE London Cocoa |
| NAV | Struck daily; $5.277 at 9 Oct 2026 |
| Hedge equivalence | 10,000 shares move like one ICE London cocoa lot (10 t) |
| Batch auctions | Every 12 days, after Genesis settlement |

A new asset is a new vault with its own lots and NAV. Every new line must pass the same [eight collateral tests](/products/real-world-assets#eight-tests-for-any-commodity).

## Before you bid

<Check>Complete KYC or KYB and whitelist your wallet. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).</Check>
<Check>Verify the contract address on the [Contract Addresses](/resources/contract-addresses) page and the deposit address by phone, never from a message.</Check>
<Check>Read the [Risk Checklist](/safety/risk-checklist) and the Risk Statement in the data room.</Check>
<Check>Check today's NAV, the lots listed for the batch and their reserves.</Check>

<Note>
  Vault shares are for eligible non-US professional or institutional investors outside any jurisdiction the vault's term sheet excludes (the CEMAC region for the Genesis Vault). Parameters can change with 30 days' notice; your signed agreement and the Rulebook are binding.
</Note>
