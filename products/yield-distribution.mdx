Yield from real-world assets backing Cargo Vaults is collected off-chain, brought on-chain, and distributed to depositors according to each vault's rules. This page covers how accrual works and what you can do with your earnings.

## Accrual and Payout Mechanics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How yield accrues">
    Yield is generated through interest payments, financing fees, and profit-sharing arrangements from underlying real-world assets.

    Returns accrue according to each asset's repayment schedule and may be reflected in the vault's Net Asset Value (NAV) as income is recognized.

    Actual yield depends on the performance of the underlying investments and is not guaranteed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payout timing">
    Yield distributions follow each vault's investment duration and underlying asset repayment schedules.

    - **90-Day Vaults:** Distributions according to the short-term investment cycle.
    - **180-Day Vaults:** Distributions according to the medium-term investment cycle.
    - **360-Day Vaults:** Distributions according to the long-term investment cycle.

    Once off-chain payments are received, verified, and reconciled, eligible returns are made available for on-chain distribution, subject to applicable fees and processing timelines.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Reinvestment options">
    Depending on the vault's configuration, depositors may have two options for managing their earnings:

    - **Auto-Compound:** Reinvest earned yield into the vault to increase capital exposure and potential future returns.
    - **Claim Yield:** Withdraw available earnings to a connected wallet without redeeming the original principal, where permitted.

    Reinvestment preferences are managed through the vault's dashboard, where supported. Available options, payout schedules, and withdrawal restrictions depend on each vault's investment terms.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Tracking Your Earnings

Cargo aims to provide depositors with visibility into vault performance through on-chain accounting and investment reporting.

Key metrics may include **current APY, accrued yield, distributed earnings, NAV, and historical performance**.

_Displayed APYs are estimates based on the applicable methodology and do not represent guaranteed returns. Off-chain settlement delays, asset performance, and liquidity conditions may affect actual distributions._
