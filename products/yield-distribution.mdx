cvCOCOA does not pay a coupon. A share is a kilogram of cocoa. Its value is NAV, and what you earn is the gap between what you paid and what you get back, plus any bonus shares.

## Sources of return

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The discount to NAV" icon="tag">
    New shares are only ever sold below NAV: 9.2% to 3.0% below on the Genesis curve, then at each batch's clearing discount. Redemption returns NAV − 0.10%.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="NAV moves" icon="chart-line">
    NAV follows the ONCC export price of cocoa, in USD. It can rise or fall. The discount you bought at is your cushion: NAV can fall by about that much, less the redemption fee, before the position is below cost. Market makers and sophisticated holders can hedge on ICE London (10,000 shares ≈ one 10 t lot).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Genesis lock bonus" icon="lock">
    Genesis shares locked for **90 days** receive **+1.0%** bonus shares; locked for **180 days**, **+2.5%**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rotate" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Buy in a batch below NAV, pre-sell the same kilos to an offtaker at NAV, redeem, and bid again. Each 12-day turn earns roughly the clearing discount less 0.35% of fees. The discount is set by the auction and will compress as more capital arrives.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What you do not pay

- No management fee and no storage fee for holders. Storage, collateral management and insurance are paid by originators at cost.
- No fee on the Genesis curve.
- Fees apply only when you trade or exit: 0.25% per side in a batch, 0.10% on redemption, 1.0% through the bridge. See the [fee schedule](/ecosystem/protocol#fee-schedule).

## Tracking your position

| Data | Where | Frequency |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| NAV | On-chain, after ONCC publishes | Daily |
| iNAV | API and WebSocket; on-chain during ICE hours | Every minute; every 15 minutes on-chain |
| Batch tape | Clearing price, discount, volume, bid-to-cover, lot IDs | Each batch |
| Lot registry | Lot ID, tonnage, grade, receipt | Per lot |
| Stock report | Collateral manager's report, hashed on-chain | Weekly |
| Redemption queue and bridge capacity | Live | Continuous |

<Note>
  Figures are at ONCC prices of 9 Oct 2026, before price moves. They are illustrations, not forecasts, and they are never annualised. Capital is at risk. See [Returns and NAV](/faq/vault-apy).
</Note>
