## Base (fallback chain)

Used only if Arc's launch gates are not met. If a vault deploys on Base, its addresses will be listed here instead, and this page will say so.

<Warning>
  Never trust an address shared in a chat, DM, email or unofficial channel. Your **deposit address** is the one in your signed agreement, confirmed by phone with a named director, and nothing else. Contract addresses must match this page and the verified source on the block explorer.
</Warning>
