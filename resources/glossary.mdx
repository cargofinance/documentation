Cargo Finance sits between the physical trade in real-world assets and DeFi. Each side has its own vocabulary. This page defines both, then the local terms used for the Genesis Vault (Cameroon cocoa).

## Commodity and trade terms

| Term | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Collateral manager** | An independent firm that controls the warehouse or vault, weighs, grades and signs for goods, and releases them only against payment |
| **Delivery costs** | The fixed costs of moving goods from storage to the point where the reference price applies (for example port, handling and insurance). They are deducted in NAV |
| **EUDR** | The EU Deforestation Regulation; it requires plot-level origin data for commodities such as cocoa, coffee and cattle products sold into the EU |
| **Export parity** | What goods are worth at the point of export, after export costs |
| **Farmgate price** | The price paid to farmers or cooperatives at origin |
| **FOB (free on board)** | A price for goods loaded on the vessel at the port of export, with the buyer paying freight from there |
| **Hedge market** | The liquid futures or benchmark market a vault's asset is checked and hedged against (for example ICE for softs, LME for base metals, LBMA for gold) |
| **Lot** | A batch of goods received into custody, with its own lot ID, weight, grade and receipt |
| **Offtaker** | A buyer (exporter, processor, refiner or trader) that buys the goods for export or processing |
| **Receipt** | The collateral manager's signed record that a lot is in custody, with its weight and grade. No receipt, no mint |
| **Reference price** | The published price a vault's NAV starts from: a national board's price, an exchange price or a benchmark |

## Legal and regulatory terms

| Term | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Limited recourse, non-petition** | Contract terms that stop creditors from claiming beyond the SPV's assets or forcing it into insolvency |
| **Pledge with dispossession** | A pledge where the goods are handed to a third party (here, the collateral manager) rather than staying with the owner |
| **Regulation S** | The US rule for offers made outside the United States. Cargo vaults are offered only to non-US persons |
| **Seychelles FSA** | The Financial Services Authority of Seychelles, which Cargo has asked to confirm how it classifies cvCOCOA |
| **SPV** | Special purpose vehicle. The bankruptcy-remote Seychelles company that owns every receipted lot in a vault |

## Vault and market terms

| Term | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Authorised participant (AP)** | A panel firm that can create shares at NAV + 0.10% and redeem at NAV − 0.10%, keeping the market price close to NAV |
| **Batch auction** | A sealed, uniform-price auction at a fixed interval in which the Vault SPV sells new shares (every 12 days in the Genesis Vault) |
| **Bonding curve** | A price that rises with the amount raised. Each Genesis Auction uses a linear curve that stops below NAV |
| **cv token** | A Cargo vault token: "cv" plus the asset, for example cvCOCOA. One share is one unit of the receipted asset |
| **Discount to NAV** | How far below NAV new shares sell. It is the main source of return for holders |
| **First loss (subordination)** | The 10% of each lot that originators keep as subordinated shares; it absorbs shortfalls before senior holders |
| **Genesis Auction** | The first sale of a vault's shares, on a bonding curve, with a minimum and a cap |
| **Genesis Vault** | The first Cargo vault: export-grade Cameroon cocoa in Douala, issued as cvCOCOA |
| **Hold / Rotate** | Hold: keep shares bought below NAV. Rotate: buy in each batch, pre-sell the units, redeem and bid again |
| **iNAV** | Indicative NAV between daily fixes, moved with the asset's hedge market |
| **Liquidity Bridge** | A pool of LP capital that buys shares instantly from holders at NAV − 1.0% |
| **NAV** | Net asset value per share: the asset's reference price, less levies and delivery costs, converted to USD |
| **Reserve price** | The lowest price an originator will accept for a lot in a batch, published before the window opens |
| **RFQ** | Request for quote: a holder asks panel market makers for firm prices on a block |
| **Tape** | The public record of each batch: clearing price, discount to NAV, volume, bid-to-cover and lot IDs |
| **UN/LOCODE** | The UN code for a port (DLA for Douala, KBI for Kribi), used to name port vaults |
| **Vault** | One asset in one location, held as receipted, pledged inventory by a Vault SPV, with its own lots, NAV and token |

## On-chain terms

| Term | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Arc** | Circle's USDC-native layer-1 chain and the target home for Cargo vault tokens. Base is the fallback |
| **Atomic settlement** | Shares and USDC swap in a single transaction, so neither side can fail to deliver |
| **ERC-3643** | A permissioned token standard: every transfer is checked against an on-chain identity registry |
| **Identity registry** | The on-chain whitelist of verified wallets. Only a participant ID and wallet status are stored, never personal data |
| **Pause guardian** | A role that can suspend minting, batches, creations or redemptions on a safety trigger |
| **Safe** | A multisignature wallet used to administer the contracts, behind a timelock |
| **Timelock** | A delay between approving an administrative change and executing it, so changes are visible first |
| **USDC** | Circle's US dollar stablecoin, the settlement asset for every trade |

## Genesis Vault terms

Terms that apply to the Genesis Vault's Cameroon cocoa and its local legal setting.

| Term | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **AGL Bonabéri** | The port terminal in Douala, Cameroon, where Genesis Vault lots are stored in a collateral-managed bay |
| **BEAC** | The central bank of the CEMAC region. Its exchange rules govern how export proceeds are repatriated |
| **CEMAC** | The Central African Economic and Monetary Community: Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon. Residents are excluded from the Genesis Vault |
| **Cut test, bean count, moisture** | Standard cocoa quality checks recorded on every Genesis Vault receipt |
| **cvCOCOA** | Cargo Vault Cocoa · Douala, the Genesis Vault token. One share is one kilogram of receipted cocoa |
| **FCFA (XAF)** | The Central African CFA franc, pegged at 655.957 per euro |
| **ICE London cocoa** | The cocoa futures contract on ICE Futures Europe. One lot is 10 tonnes; it is the hedge reference for cvCOCOA |
| **OHADA** | The Organisation for the Harmonisation of Business Law in Africa. Its Uniform Act on secured transactions governs the pledge over the Genesis Vault's cocoa |
| **ONCC** | Office National du Cacao et du Café, Cameroon's cocoa and coffee board. It publishes the daily FOB reference price that the Genesis Vault's NAV starts from |
| **RCCM** | The trade and personal property credit register where companies and pledges are recorded in OHADA countries |
