Cargo audits two things: the code that issues and trades shares, and the stock that backs them.

## Smart-contract audits

The contracts that mint, auction and hold value (the minting guard, the Genesis curve and the batch auction) will be independently audited **before they go live on mainnet**. No audit has been completed yet. Reports will be listed here as they are published.

| Auditor | Date | Scope | Report |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Not yet appointed | — | Minting, Genesis curve, batch auction | Published here |

## Stock and financial audits

| Check | Who | Cadence |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Lot verification | Independent inspector (Vallis in the Genesis Vault) | Every lot |
| Stock report hash | Collateral manager | Weekly |
| Agreed-upon procedures on stock against shares | Independent accountant | Quarterly |
| Auction log review | Independent reviewer | Quarterly |
| Financial statements | Auditor, named on signing | Annual |

## Reporting a vulnerability

A formal bug bounty with reward tiers has not launched yet. Until it does, we still want to hear from you and will credit responsible disclosures.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare">Document the issue, the steps to reproduce it and its impact.</Step>
  <Step title="Submit">Email **cargovaults@gmail.com** with the subject line "Security". Do not post details publicly.</Step>
  <Step title="Wait">Give us time to triage and fix before any disclosure. We will confirm receipt and agree a timeline with you.</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Never test against mainnet contracts or real funds. Use a local fork or a testnet.
</Info>
