## Upgrade Patterns

Strategies may be replaceable to adapt to new RWA opportunities.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Propose">
    A new strategy or parameter set is submitted on-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Queue">
    The timelock holds the proposal for the required delay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute">
    After the delay, the change is executed and becomes active.
  </Step>
</Steps>
