Every Cargo vault is administered through a Safe multisig behind a timelock, so changes are visible before they take effect.

## Ownership

- **Administration:** a Safe multisig. The v4 design specifies a 3-of-5 Safe (issuer director, second director, operations, independent director, security agent), with a guard that requires the independent director or the security agent to sign every transfer out.
- **Custody:** holders keep shares in their own wallets. The Safe administers contracts; it does not hold client assets.
- **Addresses:** published on the [Contract Addresses](/resources/contract-addresses) page only.

## Notice and Timelock

| Change | Notice |
| :-- | :-- |
| Rulebook, fees, auction parameters | 30 days |
| Risk parameters (haircuts, caps, eligible lots) | Set by the risk committee and published |
| Contract upgrades and administrative actions | Queued in the timelock before execution |
| Safety pause | Immediate |

## How a Change Is Rolled Out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Propose">The change is prepared and signed by the Safe owners.</Step>
  <Step title="Queue">It waits in the timelock and can be cancelled before it executes.</Step>
  <Step title="Execute">After the delay, it executes and the change is announced.</Step>
</Steps>

## Pauses

<Warning>
  A pause guardian can suspend a batch, creations and redemptions, or minting when the NAV oracle fails, a lot shows a collateral-manager discrepancy above 0.5%, a sanctions screen hits or the chain has an incident. Minting also stops if a collateral-manager report is stale. A pause stops new activity; it does not move holders' shares.
</Warning>
