Read this before you bid. The binding version is the Risk Statement in the data room, which every investor signs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cocoa price">
    NAV follows the ONCC export price of cocoa and can fall.

    <Info>The discount you buy at is your cushion. Holders and market makers can hedge on ICE London: 10,000 shares move like one 10 t lot.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Basis and oracle">
    ONCC's reference can diverge from ICE, ONCC can fail to publish, and the vault's realised sale prices can come in below NAV.

    <Info>Differential check at $250/t from the 30-day median with ICE fallback and a pause; outage fallback after two business days; FOB-cost reset if realised sales average $150/t below NAV over 30 days; lower-of oracle for lending.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Custody, quality and fraud">
    Goods could be missing, short-weight, off-grade or damaged.

    <Info>Independent collateral manager with sole release authority; Vallis verification of every lot; weekly reports hashed on-chain; suspension and recount above 0.5% discrepancy; all-risks insurance; 10% subordinated first loss.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Counterparty and sponsor">
    The originator, buyer, exporter of record or Nova could fail.

    <Info>The Vault SPV owns the goods, not Nova; one pledge for all holders; originators warrant weight, grade and title and top up shortfalls; physical delivery from 10 t.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Liquidity">
    You may not be able to exit when you want, at the price you want.

    <Info>Redemption at NAV − 0.10% at each batch (first at Batch 4), paid within 12 days; Liquidity Bridge at NAV − 1.0% up to its capacity; RFQ with panel market makers, with no guarantee of a bid; redemptions may be pro rata if the queue exceeds bridge capacity.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Legal and regulatory">
    Security could be harder to enforce than expected, and rules in Seychelles or CEMAC could change or be applied differently.

    <Info>Title to the SPV plus a registered OHADA pledge; counsel in Seychelles and Cameroon; private offers to eligible investors only; the Seychelles FSA has been asked to classify cvCOCOA, and the venue will move to a licensed route if required.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Smart contract and keys">
    Code can have bugs; keys can be lost; chains can halt.

    <Info>Independent audit before mainnet (not yet completed); Safe administration with timelock and independent co-signer; pause guardian; non-custodial atomic settlement; paper records as fallback.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Concentration">
    Cycle 1 is one commodity, one origin, one port.

    <Info>Ring-fenced lots; insurance; later vaults add Kribi, robusta and a second OHADA port.</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Figures in our materials are illustrations, not forecasts, and are never annualised. You can lose some or all of your capital.
</Warning>
