Read this before you bid. The risks below apply to every Cargo vault; the examples are from the Genesis Vault (cvCOCOA, Cameroon cocoa). The binding version is the Risk Statement for each vault in the data room, which every investor signs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Asset price">
    NAV follows the reference price of the underlying asset (for cvCOCOA, the ONCC export price of cocoa) and can fall.

    <Info>The discount you buy at is your cushion. Holders and market makers can hedge on the asset's reference market. In the Genesis Vault, 10,000 shares move like one 10 t ICE London cocoa lot.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Basis and oracle">
    The reference price can diverge from the hedge market, the reference can fail to publish, and the vault's realised sale prices can come in below NAV.

    <Info>Every vault has a differential check with a hedge-market fallback and a pause, an outage fallback, a cost reset when realised sales run below NAV, and a lower-of oracle for lending. Genesis Vault values: $250/t from the 30-day median against ICE; outage fallback after two business days of no ONCC price; FOB-cost reset if realised sales average $150/t below NAV over 30 days.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Custody, quality and fraud">
    Goods could be missing, short-weight, off-grade or damaged.

    <Info>Independent collateral manager with sole release authority; independent verification of every lot (Vallis in the Genesis Vault); weekly reports hashed on-chain; suspension and recount above 0.5% discrepancy; all-risks insurance; 10% subordinated first loss.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Counterparty and sponsor">
    The originator, buyer, exporter or dealer, or the sponsor (Nova for the Genesis Vault) could fail.

    <Info>The Vault SPV owns the goods, not the sponsor; one pledge for all holders; originators warrant weight, grade and title and top up shortfalls; physical delivery above a set size (10 t in the Genesis Vault).</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Liquidity">
    You may not be able to exit when you want, at the price you want.

    <Info>Redemption at NAV − 0.10% at each batch (in the Genesis Vault, first at Batch 4, paid within 12 days); Liquidity Bridge at NAV − 1.0% up to its capacity; RFQ with panel market makers, with no guarantee of a bid; redemptions may be pro rata if the queue exceeds bridge capacity.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Legal and regulatory">
    Security could be harder to enforce than expected, and rules in Seychelles or in the country where the goods are stored could change or be applied differently.

    <Info>Title to the SPV plus a registered pledge where the goods are stored; legal opinions from counsel in Seychelles and that country before a vault's first mint (Seychelles and Cameroon, under OHADA, for the Genesis Vault); private offers to eligible investors only; the Seychelles FSA has been asked to classify cvCOCOA, and the venue will move to a licensed route if required.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Smart contract and keys">
    Code can have bugs; keys can be lost; chains can halt.

    <Info>Independent audit before mainnet (not yet completed); Safe administration with timelock and independent co-signer; pause guardian; non-custodial atomic settlement; paper records as fallback.</Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Concentration">
    Each vault is one asset in one location. The Genesis Vault, the only vault at launch, is one commodity, one origin, one port.

    <Info>Ring-fenced lots; insurance; later vaults spread exposure across assets and locations, such as more ports, coffee and sugar, each only after the eight tests.</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Figures in our materials are illustrations, not forecasts, and are never annualised. You can lose some or all of your capital.
</Warning>
